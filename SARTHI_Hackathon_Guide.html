1. Executive Summary & Market Problem

1.1 The Etymology & Philosophy

SARTHI (सारथी) translates from Sanskrit to "The Trusted Charioteer / Companion" who guides one through challenging situations. In everyday Indian households, when an emergency electrical spark happens, a main water pipe bursts, or an AC fails during peak 45°C summer heat, finding an honest, skilled, and fairly-priced technician within 20 minutes is chaotic and stressful.

1.2 The Real-World Market Problem (The Pain Points)

India's blue-collar home services sector is estimated at over $50 Billion, yet more than 88% operates in the informal, unorganized sector:

🚫 1. Middlemen & 30% Commission Traps

Existing conglomerate platforms (like Urban Company) charge workers 25-35% commission and mandate rigid, overpriced packages, squeezing worker margins and inflating consumer prices.

🔍 2. Directory Chaos (JustDial/IndiaMART)

Traditional search directories bombard customers with spam phone calls from 10 agencies rather than giving direct pricing, proximity, live availability, or verified credentials.

⚠️ 3. Zero Trust & Safety Verification

Customers invite strangers into their homes without knowing if their identity is verified, if they have past complaint records, or if their skill credentials are authentic.

📉 4. Low Tech Literacy for Local Workers

Local electricians, mistris, and plumbers in Tier-2/3 cities cannot navigate complex English SaaS apps. They need a simple, visual, mobile-first one-tap interface.

1.3 The Sarthi Solution Blueprint

💡 How Sarthi Wins the Market: Sarthi establishes a direct, transparent, zero-commission bridge between local households and verified neighborhood tradesmen with AI-assisted problem diagnosis, proximity-first matching, and a 6-stage real-time job timeline.

3. Algorithmic Innovations (The "Secret Sauce")

3.1 The Smart Matchmaking & Ranking Engine (`src/lib/matching.ts`)

Unlike naive platforms that sort workers alphabetically, Sarthi calculates an intelligent Multi-Factor Match Score ($S$) for each worker candidate:

📐 Mathematical Proximity Calculation (Haversine Formula): Given customer coordinates $(\phi_1, \lambda_1)$ and worker coordinates $(\phi_2, \lambda_2)$, distance $d$ in kilometers is computed as: 
const R = 6371; // Earth's mean radius in km
const dLat = ((lat2 - lat1) * Math.PI) / 180;
const dLon = ((lon2 - lon1) * Math.PI) / 180;
const a = Math.sin(dLat / 2) ** 2 +
          Math.cos((lat1 * Math.PI) / 180) *
          Math.cos((lat2 * Math.PI) / 180) *
          Math.sin(dLon / 2) ** 2;
const distanceKm = R * 2 * Math.atan2(Math.sqrt(a), Math.sqrt(1 - a));

Multi-Factor Ranking Weights:

Factor Weight Points Rationale
Proximity (Distance) $100 - (5 \times \text{distanceKm})$ Ensures technicians who can reach the customer in under 15 minutes appear at the top.
Customer Rating (⭐) $\text{Rating} \times 8$ (up to +40 pts) Rewards highly-rated, professional workers with higher platform visibility.
Identity & Trade Verification +15 pts Prioritizes government ID (Aadhaar/PAN) and background-checked technicians.
Real-Time Availability +10 pts Gives active, on-duty workers immediate booking priority.
24x7 Emergency Capability +10 pts (when emergency active) Surfaces rapid-response technicians during night hours or crisis breakdowns.

3.2 The AI Problem Diagnostician (`src/lib/ai-assistant.ts`)

Most non-technical customers do not know whether a sparking ceiling fan requires a "Capacitor Replacement", a "Switchboard MCB Overhaul", or an "Appliance Repair".

Sarthi integrates a Natural Language Intent & Safety Diagnostician that:

  • Parses Plain Description: Reads conversational Hindi/English sentences (e.g. "Pani ka pipe toot gaya hai bathroom me" or "Fan regulator is making sparking noise").
  • Extracts Trade & Service: Identifies category (Plumber/Electrician) and suggests exact repair scope.
  • Calculates Price Benchmark: Displays estimated labor price range (₹199 – ₹450) so customers are never overcharged.
  • Proactive Safety Warnings: Issues critical precautions (e.g. "⚠️ Turn off the main circuit breaker before touching any switches").

7. Top 20 Toughest Hackathon Judge Questions & Answers

Q1 How is Sarthi different from Urban Company?
Answer: Urban Company focuses primarily on metro Tier-1 cities, charges workers heavy 25-35% platform cuts, and forces standardized high-cost packages. Sarthi is tailored for Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities (Bharat) with a zero-commission model, direct customer-to-worker connection, on-demand emergency trades (plumbing, masons, welders, mechanics), and an AI assistant that understands vernacular symptoms.
Q2 How is Sarthi different from JustDial / Sulekha?
Answer: JustDial is a static phone directory that sells customer phone numbers to 10 competing contractors who spam the user. Sarthi provides real-time availability, upfront rate cards, distance-based ranking, in-app messaging without exposing personal numbers, and a 6-stage live audit timeline for the entire job.
Q3 How does the AI Problem Diagnostician work? Is it an external API or custom logic?
Answer: Sarthi features a modular Natural Language Engine (`src/lib/ai-assistant.ts`). It performs tokenized keyword intent extraction, sentiment urgency scoring, and rule-based fallback classification. It is architected so it can run entirely client-side with zero API costs, and seamlessly plug into Google Gemini / OpenAI LLMs when API keys are configured.
Q4 How do you prevent workers and customers from bypassing your platform after the first job?
Answer: We align incentives through our "Sarthi Trust & Growth Flywheel":
  • For Workers: Every completed job logged in Sarthi increases their verified job count, boosts their smart search ranking, and earns them trust badges that win them 10x more high-paying clients.
  • For Customers: Bookings through Sarthi include safety verification, timeline audit protection, and access to dispute resolution.
Q5 How do you verify worker background and skills?
Answer: Sarthi has a 3-tier verification pipeline managed through the Admin Command Center: 1. Identity Check: Aadhaar / Government ID verification. 2. Trade Credential / Reference Check: Past experience verification and local trade association reference. 3. Community Vetting: Workers start with a "New Pro" badge; once they complete 5 jobs with >4.5⭐ rating, they are elevated to "Verified Master Pro".
Q6 What security measures did you implement in this codebase?
Answer: We built enterprise-grade security practices:
  • Bcrypt Password Hashing: Salt-based hashing with 10 rounds.
  • Stateless Signed JWT Sessions: Managed with `jose` and stored in `SameSite=Lax`, `HttpOnly` secure cookies.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Server-side authorization middleware guards (`requireRole(['ADMIN', 'WORKER', 'CUSTOMER'])`).
  • Zod Schema Validation: Rejects malformed or malicious payload injections.
  • Parameterized Database Queries: Prisma ORM query engine prevents SQL injection vulnerabilities completely.
Q7 What is your business and monetization model?
Answer: 1. Worker Premium Membership (₹199/month): Top listing placement, priority emergency leads, and verified pro badge. 2. Micro-Insurance Fee (₹10/job): Accidental damage protection on booked jobs. 3. Featured Enterprise / Commercial Leads: Bulk electrical and plumbing contracts for apartment complexes and offices.
Q8 How does Sarthi work for workers who cannot read or write English?
Answer: Sarthi is designed mobile-first with high-contrast colored icon cards, visual status toggles (Green = Available, Red = Busy), and simple audio/haptic cues. Our Phase 2 roadmap includes vernacular voice input (Hindi, Bhojpuri, Tamil) using Web Speech API.
